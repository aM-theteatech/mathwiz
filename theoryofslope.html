One Straight Line, Three Different Forms

"The equation changes, but the line stays the same!"

General Form
Standard Structure:
ax + by = c
Anchor Example:
2x + 4y = 8
• Useful for quick algebra & moving terms.
Gradient-Intercept
Standard Structure:
y = mx + c
Anchor Example:
y = -12x + 2
• Shows slope m = -½ & y-intercept c = 2.
Intercept Form
Standard Structure:
xa + yb = 1
Anchor Example:
x4 + y2 = 1
• Shows x-intercept a = 4 & y-intercept b = 2.
💡
Avoid Symbol Confusion: The letter c in General Form (ax + by = c) is a constant (here = 8), while c in Gradient-Intercept Form (y = mx + c) represents the y-intercept value (here = 2). Also, a in Intercept Form is the x-intercept (4), not the coefficient of x!

📐 Slope Triangle Breakdown: m = -½

Gradient m = Change in y / Change in x = -1 / +2. Starting at (0,2), move 2 units RIGHT → then 1 unit DOWN ↓ to reach (2,1) or (4,0) on the exact same line!
